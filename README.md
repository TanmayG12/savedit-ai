# SavedIt

SavedIt keeps the links, posts, videos, products, places and ideas you want to return to in one calm library. Connect it to Cursor and you can search that library in plain language, without digging through ten different apps.

Ask it to find the recipe you saved last week, the article about a topic you half remember, or everything you saved from one creator. It can open the details of a save, count your saves, and browse your collections, including collections other people shared with you.

You can also ask it to act on your library: save a new link with a note or tags, edit a title, notes or tags, create or rename a collection, add or remove saves from a collection, and invite people to a collection you own. Deleting a save or a collection, or revoking someone's access, only happens when you ask for it clearly.

Everything stays tied to your own SavedIt account. It only sees your own saves and collections, never another person's. You can disconnect at any time from Cursor or from SavedIt Settings.

## Use it in Cursor

1. Install SavedIt from the Cursor Marketplace.
2. Sign in to SavedIt when Cursor opens the browser.
3. Ask Cursor: "find the article I saved about ..."

Server: `https://mcp.saveditapp.com`
